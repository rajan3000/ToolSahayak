Welcome to Toolaxa. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored and protected when you visit or use our website at https://toolaxa.com/. We aim to be transparent about our website practices and the information associated with using our online tools. Please read this policy carefully.

1. Introduction

Toolaxa provides online utilities and digital tools designed to help users complete everyday tasks. Our tools may include calculators, image utilities, document-related tools and other browser-based services.

Basic tools may be available without creating a user account. This Privacy Policy applies to information handled in connection with your use of the website.

By using Toolaxa, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with the practices described here, please discontinue using the website.

2. Information We Collect

Toolaxa does not currently require users to create an account to access its basic online tools.

Depending on how you interact with our website, the following types of information may be processed:

  • Contact information: If you email us, we may receive your name, email address and the contents of your message.
  • Technical information: Our hosting provider and technical services may process information such as IP address, browser type, device information and server logs.
  • Usage information: Analytics services may collect information about website visits, page views, browser interactions and general usage patterns.

The information collected depends on the services enabled and your interaction with the website.

3. Image and Signature Tools

Toolaxa provides image utilities such as Image Compressor, Photo Resizer and Signature Maker.

Tools designed for local browser processing may process selected files directly on your device without intentionally uploading them to our servers during normal operation.

Actual file handling may vary depending on the individual tool and its implementation. Please review the instructions provided within each tool before processing files.

  • Keep a backup of important original files.
  • Do not use untrusted or shared devices for sensitive documents.
  • Only upload or process files that you have the right to use.
  • Check the individual tool's functionality before processing confidential information.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small text files that websites may store in your browser. They can help websites remember preferences, support functionality and understand website usage.

Toolaxa and its integrated third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies for purposes such as:

  • Website functionality and performance.
  • Analytics and traffic measurement.
  • Advertising, if advertising services are enabled.
  • Security and technical operations.

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website features.

5. Google Analytics and Website Analytics

Toolaxa uses Google Analytics to understand website traffic and improve the user experience.

Google Analytics may process information such as page views, browser and device details, approximate location derived from technical information, and interactions with website pages.

Analytics technologies may use cookies or similar identifiers, depending on configuration and browser settings.

To learn more about how Google processes information, please review Google's Privacy Policy .

6. Third-Party Services and Advertising

Toolaxa may use third-party providers for website hosting, analytics, security, advertising and other technical services.

If Google AdSense or another advertising service is enabled, advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to display advertisements, measure performance or provide relevant ads, subject to their own policies and settings.

Third-party providers process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

7. How We Use Information

Information handled through Toolaxa may be used for legitimate website and operational purposes, including:

  • Responding to questions, feedback and support requests.
  • Maintaining website functionality.
  • Understanding website traffic and usage patterns.
  • Improving tools and user experience.
  • Identifying errors and technical problems.
  • Protecting the website against misuse and security threats.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations.

8. Sharing of Information

Toolaxa does not sell personal information as part of its stated website practices.

Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to operate, maintain, secure or improve the website. Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or a valid legal request.

Third-party services are responsible for their own processing practices under their respective privacy policies.

9. Data Security

We aim to use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information handled through Toolaxa.

However, no method of internet transmission, website operation or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should avoid sending confidential information through unsecured channels.

10. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to respond to requests, maintain website operations, meet legitimate business requirements or comply with applicable legal obligations.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the service provider involved.

11. Children's Privacy

Toolaxa is a general-purpose website and is not specifically directed at children.

We do not knowingly request personal information from children for account registration. Parents or guardians who believe that a child has provided personal information to us may contact us for assistance.

12. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights regarding personal information associated with you. These may include requesting access, correction or deletion of information, where applicable.

You may also manage cookies through your browser settings and review the privacy controls offered by third-party services.

To submit a privacy-related request, please contact us using the details provided below.

13. External Websites and Links

Our website may contain links to external websites or services for additional information and convenience.

Toolaxa does not control the privacy practices, security or content of external websites. We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any third-party website you visit.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in website features, third-party services or applicable requirements.

Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date. We encourage users to review this page periodically.
