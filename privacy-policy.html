Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Learn how Toolaxa handles information when you use our website.

Last Updated: 12 November 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Toolaxa. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used and protected when you visit or use our website.

By using Toolaxa, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy. Please do not use the website if you do not agree with its terms.

2. Information We Collect

Toolaxa does not currently require users to create an account to use its basic tools.

When you contact us by email, we may receive information such as your name, email address and the contents of your message.

Our hosting provider or other technical services may process standard technical information, such as browser details, IP address and server logs, depending on the services configured.

3. Image and Signature Tools

Our Image Compressor, Photo Resizer and Signature Maker are designed to process files directly within your browser.

These tools do not intentionally upload your selected images or signatures to our server during normal local processing.

Please remember that downloaded files are saved on your own device. We recommend avoiding sensitive personal documents on devices you do not trust.

Cookies are small files that websites may store in your browser.

Toolaxa may use cookies or similar technologies in the future for website functionality, analytics, advertising and performance measurement.

If such services are introduced, this policy will be updated to describe their use.

Toolaxa may use third-party services such as website hosting, analytics providers and advertising partners in the future.

If Google AdSense or other advertising services are enabled, third-party vendors may use cookies or similar technologies to display or measure advertisements.

These third-party services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

6. How We Use Information

Information received by Toolaxa may be used for purposes such as:

  • Responding to user questions and feedback.
  • Maintaining and improving website functionality.
  • Identifying technical problems.
  • Protecting the website against misuse.
  • Complying with applicable legal requirements.

7. Data Security

We aim to use reasonable measures to protect information handled through our website. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Toolaxa is a general-purpose website. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for the purpose of creating user accounts.

Parents or guardians can contact us if they believe a child has provided personal information through our website.
