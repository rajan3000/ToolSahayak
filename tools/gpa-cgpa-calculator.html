What is GPA?

GPA (Grade Point Average) represents a student's average academic performance for a particular semester.

GPA = Sum of (Grade Point × Credits) ÷ Total Credits

What is CGPA?

CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) represents a student's overall academic performance across multiple semesters.

CGPA = Sum of (Semester GPA × Semester Credits) ÷ Total Credits

Important Note

GPA and CGPA grading systems vary between universities and institutions. Enter the correct grade points and credits according to your official academic rules.

Percentage conversion using 9.5 is only a commonly used example. Your university may prescribe a different conversion formula.
