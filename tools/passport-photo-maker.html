How to Make Passport Size Photos?

  4. Use AI Remove Background if you need to replace an existing background.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this Passport Photo Maker free?

Yes. You can create and download passport-size photos without registration or payment.

Does it support a white background?

Yes. The tool uses a white photo background. For an image with an existing colored background, run AI Remove Background first.

Can I print multiple photos on one page?

Yes. Create an A4 sheet with your selected number of copies and download or print it.

Are my photos uploaded to a server?

Photo editing and sheet creation happen in your browser. The optional AI background-removal library and model are downloaded from an external CDN. Your selected photo is not intentionally uploaded by this page.

Will this photo be accepted for every passport or visa application?

Not necessarily. Photo size, face position, background, expression and other requirements vary by country and application. Check the official photo specifications before submitting.
