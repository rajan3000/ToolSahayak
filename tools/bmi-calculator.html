What is BMI?

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a measurement that uses a person's height and weight to estimate whether their weight is within a healthy range. It is commonly used as an initial screening tool for adults.

BMI Categories for Adults

BMI Range Category
Below 18.5Underweight
18.5 – 24.9Normal Weight
25.0 – 29.9Overweight
30.0 and aboveObesity

How is BMI Calculated?

BMI is calculated by dividing weight in kilograms by the square of height in metres.

BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ Height (m)²

Who Should Not Use Adult BMI Categories?

  • Children and teenagers under 20 years.
  • Pregnant individuals.
  • People whose body composition needs specialist assessment.
  • Individuals with unusually high muscle mass.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is a normal BMI?

For most adults, a BMI from 18.5 to 24.9 is classified as the normal weight range.

2. Is BMI an accurate measure of health?

BMI is a useful screening measure, but it does not directly measure body fat or overall health. Other factors are important.

3. Can I calculate BMI using feet and inches?

Yes. Select Imperial units and enter your height in feet and inches and weight in pounds.

4. What is the healthy weight range?
