What is Force?

Force is a push or pull that can change the motion or shape of an object. Its SI unit is Newton (N).

Force Formula

  • Force: F = m × a
  • Mass: m = F ÷ a
  • Acceleration: a = F ÷ m

Where F = Force, m = Mass and a = Acceleration.
