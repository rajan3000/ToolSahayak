About Unit Converter

A unit converter helps you convert measurements from one unit to another. Toolaxa's free online unit converter supports common measurement categories for everyday, educational and professional use.

Available Conversion Categories

  • Length: Millimeter, centimeter, meter, kilometer, inch, foot, yard and mile.
  • Weight: Milligram, gram, kilogram, metric tonne, ounce and pound.
  • Temperature: Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin.
  • Area: Square meter, square kilometer, hectare, acre and square foot.
  • Volume: Milliliter, liter, cubic meter, US gallon, US quart and US cup.
  • Speed: Meter/second, kilometer/hour, mile/hour, knot and foot/second.
  • Time: Millisecond, second, minute, hour, day, week and year.
  • Data Storage: Bit, byte, decimal KB/MB/GB/TB and binary KiB/MiB/GiB/TiB.
  • Pressure: Pascal, kilopascal, bar, atmosphere, PSI and mmHg.

How to Use the Unit Converter?

  • Select the conversion category.
  • Enter the value you want to convert.
  • Select the original unit and target unit.
  • Your converted result appears automatically.
  • Use the swap button to reverse the units.
  • Click Copy Result to copy the conversion.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is Toolaxa Unit Converter free?

Yes. It is free to use without registration or subscription.

2. Does the converter work on mobile?

Yes. The interface is responsive and works on mobile phones, tablets and desktop devices.

3. Can I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

Yes. Select Temperature, choose Celsius as the original unit and Fahrenheit as the target unit.

4. Does this tool need an internet connection?

After the page has loaded, conversions are calculated directly in your browser and do not need an internet connection.

5. Does it support currency conversion?

No. Currency rates change frequently. A separate live currency converter is more suitable for exchange rates.

6. Are the conversion results rounded?

Results are displayed with suitable decimal precision. Very large or very small values may be shown in scientific notation.
