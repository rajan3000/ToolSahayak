Split PDF Online Free

Extract selected pages or split one PDF into multiple PDF files easily.

Choose Pages

Total Pages: 0 Selected: 0
Enter page numbers separated by commas. Example: 1-3, 5, 7-9. Applying a range replaces the current selection.

Choose Split Method

Each range becomes a separate PDF. Use commas between ranges.
Example: Enter 5 to create PDFs with 5 pages each.
🔒 Your PDF is processed in your browser and is not uploaded to our server.

How to Split PDF Files?

  1. Upload one PDF file.
  2. Select the pages you want or enter page ranges.
  3. Choose your preferred split method.
  4. Enter an output file name.
  5. Click Split PDF & Download.
  6. Your new PDF or ZIP file will be downloaded.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this Split PDF tool free?

Yes. You can use this PDF splitter without registration or payment.

Can I extract selected pages?

Yes. Select individual pages or enter page ranges to create a new PDF.

Can I download every page separately?

Yes. Choose Extract Every Page. All pages will be saved as individual PDF files in a ZIP archive.

Are my PDF files uploaded to a server?

No. PDF processing happens inside your browser. External libraries are loaded from CDNs.

Can I use this tool on mobile?

Yes. The tool is designed for desktop, tablet and mobile browsers.

Are password-protected PDFs supported?

Password-protected or damaged PDF files may not be supported.
