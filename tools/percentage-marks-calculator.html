How to Calculate Percentage?

Use the following formula:

Percentage = (Marks Obtained ÷ Total Marks) × 100

Example: If you score 450 out of 500, your percentage is 90%.

How is Grade Calculated?

  • 90% – 100%: A+
  • 80% – 89.99%: A
  • 70% – 79.99%: B+
  • 60% – 69.99%: B
  • 50% – 59.99%: C
  • 40% – 49.99%: D
  • Below 40%: F

Note: Actual grading rules may differ between schools, boards and universities.
