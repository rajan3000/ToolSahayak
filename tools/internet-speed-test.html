About Internet Speed Test

Understand your internet performance.

↓ Download Speed

Measures how quickly your device receives data from the internet. Higher Mbps generally means faster downloading and streaming.

↑ Upload Speed

Measures how quickly your device sends data to the internet. Important for video calls, cloud backups and file sharing.

◷ Ping

Measures the response time between your device and the test server. Lower ping generally means a faster response.

≈ Jitter

Measures variation in response times. Lower jitter generally provides a more stable connection for calls and gaming.
