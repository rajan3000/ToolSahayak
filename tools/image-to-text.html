How to Convert Image to Text?

  • Choose Images from your device or click Take Photo.
  • You can upload multiple images together.
  • Click Extract Text from All Images.
  • OCR will process original and enhanced image versions.
  • Review the main result and alternative OCR results.
  • Copy individual text or copy all extracted text.
  • Download the extracted text as a TXT file.

About Hindi OCR

OCR stands for Optical Character Recognition. It converts text visible in images into editable text. This tool supports Hindi and English. Recognition quality depends on image resolution, lighting, text clarity, font style and document condition. Printed text generally gives better results than handwritten text.

Privacy

Image processing is performed in your browser using the OCR library. Images are not intentionally uploaded to a Toolaxa server by this page. The OCR engine may download its language data from the Tesseract CDN.
