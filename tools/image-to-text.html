How to Convert Image to Text?

  • Click Choose Images or drag and drop your photos.

  • Click Extract Text from All Images.
  • Review and edit the extracted Hindi or English text.
  • Copy individual text or copy all extracted text.
  • Download all extracted text in a TXT file.

About Hindi OCR

OCR stands for Optical Character Recognition. It helps convert text visible in images into editable text. This tool supports Hindi and English OCR. Results depend on image quality, font clarity, lighting and handwriting style. Printed text generally gives better results than handwritten text.
